Revoke {{ cert.domain }} at Let's Encrypt

Revoking publishes the certificate's serial to LE's CRL/OCSP responders, so browsers / engines that check revocation will stop trusting it. This cannot be undone — you'll need to issue a fresh certificate to serve the same domain.

Revoking is distinct from Stop tracking (which just removes FEA's record but leaves the cert valid at LE until natural expiry). Use the checkbox below to chain both.

RFC 5280 reason code. LE accepts this as the --reason value to certbot revoke; it surfaces on CRL entries.
